LitViz Sandbox

Synthetic data · no model · runs in your browser

LitViz turns a folder of PDFs into a map of how papers relate. A local model drafts claims and links, and nothing reaches the map unless its supporting quote is found word for word in the paper. This sandbox runs that check for real, on five made-up papers. The model output is scripted and includes deliberate mistakes. Run the pipeline, then edit a page, a quote or the grounding mode and watch what survives.

The quote check in this page is ported from LitViz's pipeline/verify.py. It ignores PDF artefacts (ligatures, hyphens at line breaks, curly quotes, dash variants, spacing, case) and nothing else. A single changed word is a rejection. Edits stay in this tab and are not sent anywhere.
